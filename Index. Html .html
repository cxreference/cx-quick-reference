Core Interaction Flow

Welcome → Identify → Understand → Acknowledge → Assist → Recap → Close

Keep the interaction clear, professional, conversational, and focused on the customer's request.

Useful Transition Phrases

Absolutely. Let me take a look at that for you.
I'd be happy to check that for you.
Let me review those details so I can give you the most accurate information.
One moment while I take a closer look into that for you.
Acknowledge → Impact → Ownership → Action
Tip: Use a specific acknowledgement and then move naturally into action. Avoid repeating the same empathy statement.

Delivery Delay

I can certainly understand how frustrating it is to be expecting your order and have the delivery timing change. Let me take a look and see what options we have.

Damaged Item

I'm sorry your item arrived damaged, especially after waiting for it to arrive. Let's get this taken care of for you.

Frustrated Customer

I can hear how frustrating this situation has been, and I appreciate you bringing it to our attention. Let me review the details and see what I can do from here.

Previous Contact

I'm sorry you've had to spend additional time reaching out about this. Let me take a closer look so I can provide you with the most accurate information.

Important Date

I can certainly see how important it is to have this in time. Let me review the details and see what options are available.

Unavailable Item

I can appreciate the disappointment of finding out the item is currently unavailable. Let me check what options we have.

Long Wait

I appreciate your patience while you've been waiting. Let me check the latest status and see what options are available.

Cancellation

I understand you no longer need the order. Let me review the details and see what options are available.

Long-Distance Delivery

Order Handling

New Order

I'd be happy to help you place an order. May I have the item or SKU number for what you'd like to purchase?

Order Status

Let me check the current status of that order for you.

Cancellation

Let me review the order status first and see what cancellation options are available.

Replacement

I'd be happy to help with a replacement. Let me review the order details and the applicable options.

Backordered / Unavailable

The item is currently unavailable. Let me check the latest estimated availability and see what options are available.

Returns & Refunds

Return Request

I'd be happy to review the return options for you. Let me first check the order and item eligibility.

Refund

Once the return is processed according to the applicable policy, the refund will be issued according to the applicable payment and refund process.

Return Policy

Let me verify the current return policy and the eligibility for this particular item before I advise you further.

Possible Fee

Depending on the item and eligibility, a fee or other condition may apply. Let me verify the specific details for your order.

Customer Communication

Clarifying Question

Just to make sure I have this correct, you're looking for [Request / Solution]. Is that right?

Need More Information

To make sure I give you the correct information, may I confirm [Information Needed]?

Hold / Research

May I place you on a brief hold while I review that information?

Returning From Hold

Thank you for holding. I appreciate your patience.

When You Don't Have the Answer Yet

I want to make sure I provide you with accurate information, so let me verify that before I give you an answer.

Recap

Just to make sure I have everything correct, you're looking for [Solution], and the key detail is [Key Detail]. Is that correct?
To recap, I've [Action Taken], and the current next step is [Next Step].
So, we've covered [Summary]. The next step will be [Next Step].

Closing

Is there anything else I can assist you with today?
